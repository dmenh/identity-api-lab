# Licensing options

The included `LICENSE` reserves all rights to Dinesh and allows evaluation only under its stated terms. It is a proprietary, source-available license rather than an open-source license because modification and redistribution are restricted.

Before publishing, choose the business model that matches how other developers should use the repository:

1. **Private commercial source:** keep the repository private and grant access under customer or evaluation agreements.
2. **Source-available evaluation:** publish the source with the existing license so people can inspect and locally evaluate it but cannot redistribute it.
3. **Open-core:** publish selected components under an OSI-approved license and keep hosted operations, enterprise controls or premium adapters commercial.
4. **Hosted service:** keep the implementation private and sell isolated test environments with usage, retention and support tiers.

For broad community contribution, replace the proprietary terms with a recognized open-source license and add contribution terms. For commercial distribution, obtain legal review for the intended countries, trademark plan, dependency notices, privacy policy and hosted service terms.
